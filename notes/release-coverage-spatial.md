Sources read and cited: https://sdmtmb.github.io/sdmTMB/articles/basic-intro.html, https://sdmtmb.github.io/sdmTMB/articles/delta-models.html, https://sdmtmb.github.io/sdmTMB/articles/residual-checking.html, https://sdmtmb.github.io/sdmTMB/reference/predict.sdmTMB.html. Fits use all 2143 Pacific cod tows, 9 survey years, a 10 km mesh, persistent spatial fields and independent year-specific spatial fields in both delta components. Adaptations are explicit: year factors, k = 6, mesh cutoff (delta source uses 15; basic intro uses 10).

Scope limits, not claimed as separately worked examples: factor-by bases, RTMB backend, grouped/time-varying/spatially varying covariate effects, Poisson-link coupled deltas and all other supported observation families are not each refitted here. Their shared behavior is described by the references and implementation tests. The main worked example covers two-component non-Gaussian support and meaningful spatial plus spatiotemporal effects. Mesh/basis refinement and comparison to delta-lognormal are recommended analysis steps but not claimed to have been done.

The tensor extension uses the official introduction's full-survey Bernoulli encounter model and actual spatial/year fields, replacing its quadratic depth formula with a joint t2(depth, year_centered) surface. Its three separately penalised parts are demonstrated using the public smooth accessor and plotting/evaluation methods. The real data give a nearly time-stable depth shape and a native smoothing-parameter warning, which is retained and interpreted. No prior or covariance workaround is added.
