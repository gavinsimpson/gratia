Findings and corrections:
- Finite source draws can have an overflowing empirical covariance. This now produces a clear error.
- Very small target variation around a large coefficient mean can disappear in floating-point arithmetic.
  Nonfinite transformations and achieved covariance errors above 1e-7 relative to the largest target entry are rejected.
- Comparison CSV rows previously carried source-MH acceptance and mean-shift fields without identifying their source.
  Six renamed columns now distinguish source_mh fields and adjustment fields from the sampler's own diagnostics.
- Missing CSV columns can produce empty logical comparisons in R. Required schemas are now checked explicitly;
  an intentionally removed diagnostic field was rejected by the negative validation check.
- Trailing blank lines were removed. No public function, signature, sampling target, export or documentation topic changed.
