Review of native MCMC diagnostic plots, 15 October 2026.
Branch: gls/posterior-chains; implementation reviewed against 0782c9738.
All fitting, tests and package checks ran outside the sandbox.

Findings and corrections:
- Unnamed coefficient matrices could retain meaningful names in diagnostic metadata,
  but the plotting accessor discarded those names. It now recovers labels only
  when they match the complete returned coefficient matrix.
- Valid retained-iteration identifiers above the R integer limit were coerced to NA.
  Native plots now preserve their numeric values. Ordinary integer IDs keep their type.
- Rank-plot documentation now describes the dashed reference as the average bin count.
  It does not claim an exact expectation for every finite bin layout.
- Terminal blank lines were removed; unrelated roxygen regeneration edits were restored.

Regression evidence:
961 focused assertions passed, with no failures, warnings or skips.
These cover plotting, chain conversion, LSS workflows, sampling interfaces and MH adjustment.
18 comparisons against the pre-plotting sampling_finish() preserved exact coefficient draws,
coefficient summaries, chain IDs, acceptance rates and caller RNG state.
Cases included Gaussian and Poisson GAMs, BAM, gaulss, both Gaussian generators,
and every supported MH backend among mgcv, gratia and family.
The trace and rank plot data for both examples shown to the user are exactly unchanged.
Large-iteration plot construction and building preserve caller RNG state and kind.

Full package checks:

Its full suite had 18712 passes, 197 skips, no failures and no warnings.
The full check on the final source completed with Status: 1 ERROR.

The failure was the existing test-scalable-gjrm.R NBII fitted-density comparison
(m$fit$pdf2 against the analytic negative-binomial mass). The warnings arose
inside GJRM fitting, including replacement-length and convergence warnings.
The same GJRM file then passed in isolation: 136 assertions, no warnings or failures.
Its family assertions also passed under Mersenne-Twister and L'Ecuyer-CMRG.
No GJRM source or test was changed. The full-run fitting failure remains unresolved;
the current full check must not be described as clean.
Installation, namespace and documentation checks, ordinary and donttest examples,
vignette checks and vignette rebuilding passed on the final source.
