Scientific limits:
Weak-Gaussian smooth-derivative coverage remains below nominal after adjustment.
Rare-binary adjusted MH improves coverage over conditional MH but is below unconditional Gaussian on the reported quantities.
Exact matching of empirical covariance does not validate posterior coverage or specify an unconditional MH target.
The transformation retains the conditional coefficient mean and depends on fitted coefficient order and finite MH covariance estimation.
Some directions can shrink. R-hat and ESS do not account for uncertainty in the estimated transformation.
The first-five resolution run changed adjusted endpoints by 0.66% of interval width on average and 8.9% at the worst point.
No public method, smoothing integration, new prior, or joint nuisance-parameter draws were added.
