Posterior sampling integration, response performance and statistical extensions
15 October 2026; gls/posterior-chains

Integrated main through b8c9fafa2 in merge commits c8c123508 and 7aab22669.
The existing posterior worktree was reused; the primary checkout was not edited.
New-main tests were migrated to n_draws and joint smooth draws. Multinomial
scale handling, fitting offsets, expression environments, discrete row restoration
and inequality warnings/errors were retained alongside the LSS expectation adapters.

Implementation:
- LSS response generation caches design blocks, retains the original matrix-vector
  products and one family RNG call per draw, fills responses directly and builds
  one output table. Streaming diagnostic sampling shares the numeric generator.
- Grouped posterior responses now accept supplied data with recoverable or explicit
  family_index membership. They retain component links, nuisance parameters, offsets,
  weights, missing rows, chain/iteration/global draw IDs and coefficient diagnostics.
  Grouped derivatives retain membership variables while constructing shifted data.
- Both prediction contexts share prediction_offsets(), including prescribed smooth
  values. Actual fitting offsets are retained exactly; ignored general-family call
  offsets are not invented from a model-frame column.
- Real soap-film fits revealed a native BAM fitting/prediction inconsistency for
  nonzero fixed contributions. MH and the smoothing prototype reject affected fits
  precisely. Gaussian approximation and supplied-draw prediction retain native
  conventions. Blockwise targets use a bounded probe, without a full dense design.
- The separate internal Gaussian smoothing prototype caches weighted crossproducts
  and recomputes conditional means/covariances. It verifies linked/fixed smoothing
  mappings and fitted coefficient coordinates, and keeps dispersion fixed.
  It is a mixture over an approximate distribution of fitted log-smoothing estimates,
  without specified hyperpriors or a joint posterior MCMC method. It remains internal.

Benchmarks:
- Final paired study: 7 LSS families, 3 seeds, 5 repetitions, 1000 draws/four chains
  at 200 new rows. All 210 timed outputs match the earlier calculation exactly.
  Median paired speedups range from 2.32 to 5.93. gaulss R allocation traffic falls
  from 66.23 MB to 52.32 MB (about 21%); output size is unchanged. This measures
  allocation traffic, not peak resident memory. See response-report.md.
- Refreshed all 21 LSS prediction cases, six grouped cases and six gaulss MH cases.
  Failures/warnings remain visible in raw results; every refreshed transformation passed.
- Longer study: 45 successful runs, three targets/five samplers/three seeds, 16000
  retained draws/four chains, 4000 burn-in per chain. All diagnostic screens pass.
  Worst coefficient/derived R-hat: 1.005415/1.004367; minimum tail ESS: 403/455.
  MALA improves tail throughput on two targets but loses bulk throughput; alternatives
  remain internal. These timings compare current R prototypes with compiled MH loops.
- The longer study uses frozen c8c12350. A separate old/final installation comparison
  verifies three fitted targets and nine MH results exactly, including coefficients,
  draw identities and acceptance information. No sampler numerical changes occurred
  for those targets. See difficult-report.md and difficult-validation.txt.
- Smoothing study: seven supported fits, 20000 draws each; independent fixed-sp refits,
  numerical integration and total-variance calculations verify the implementation.
  See smoothing-report.md and smoothing-fixed-contributions.md for restrictions.
