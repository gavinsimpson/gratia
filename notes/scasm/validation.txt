Review against main
  confint() previously rebuilt bootstrap limits from their missing critical
  values. It now shifts and transforms the stored limits directly, and uses
  the scasm default for overall uncertainty. Regression tests cover native
  limits, a decreasing transformation, the Gaussian override and warnings.
  add_confint() previously replaced bootstrap limits with Gaussian limits but
  kept their bootstrap metadata. It now updates the critical values, source,
  correction and coverage. Tests also cover replacing simultaneous bands.
  Formula offsets, locally supplied drawing arguments, and empty or omitted
  factor-level derivative evaluations passed focused review reproductions.

Windows and Linux were not available locally. Their existing CI jobs will
exercise the new tests when the branch is submitted.

Upstream reproductions
  mgcv-projection-reprex.R reproduced the empty-block indexing error in
  installed mgcv 1.9-4. The inspected development ZHZtproj() function labelled
  1.9-5 also reproduced it; the full development package was not built.
  mgcv-tensor-reprex.R showed no fitted inequalities for increasing ti()
  and t2() margins, with fitted decreases of about -0.00043 and -0.50.
  The corresponding email drafts explain the confirmed indexing bug and
  the silently accepted unsupported tensor requests. Neither was sent.
